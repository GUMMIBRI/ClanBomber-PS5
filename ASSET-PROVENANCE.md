commit `99f65141be925419ce1143cf40542eb009d5d749`. The port's README
identifies ClanBomber2 as GPL-2.0-or-later and identifies the bundled
DejaVu font license separately. The GPL text and LICENSE.DEJAVU accompany
the asset tree.

are outside the staged asset tree.
